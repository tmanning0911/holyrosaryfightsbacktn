## Education and the dissertation (read this before you slogan it)

Saint Paul University (ecclesiastical faculty) + University of Ottawa (civil) is a standard dual-degree track. That is why the diocese now styles him **J.C.D., Ph.D.** — one doctoral project, two diplomas. It is not a second, unrelated Ph.D. in another field.

**Thesis (2025)**  
*The Presumption of Imputability and the Presumption of Innocence: Two Presumptions in Conflict?*  
Saint Paul University Faculty of Canon Law thesis list, 2025.  
uOttawa repository: https://ruor.uottawa.ca/items/52c84f4a-9ce2-48bb-8a37-49f8102f01c0  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.20381/ruor-31192  
Handle on his LinkedIn: http://hdl.handle.net/10393/50581  

**What the abstract actually argues (plain English):**  
Canon 1321 of the 1983 Code says (1) a person is considered innocent until the contrary is proved, and (4) once an *external* violation of a penal law is proved, **imputability is presumed**. Some canonists say those two ideas fight. Clark’s dissertation says they do **not**. He argues the “presumption of innocence” is not a true legal presumption (a guess about an unknown fact) but a **favour of the law / regulatory precept** sitting *above* the burden of proof. He also argues this is compatible even with the older, harsher **presumption of dolus** (malice) from the 1917 Code, and that dolus “remains implicitly” in the 1983 Code.

That is a technical penal-canon-law thesis. It is public. It is **not** a confession, a smoking gun, or proof of anything about Darren Mullis. Do not quote it as “he thinks accused people are guilty.” If you use it at all, use it as: this is the legal mind of the man who is both pastor and judicial vicar.

Public teaching (undated slide deck, third-party host): “What Makes a Marriage Invalid? Very Rev. James M. Clark, J.C.L., J.V., Diocese of Memphis.” Standard tribunal catechesis: a valid, consummated marriage of two baptized Christians cannot be dissolved by any earthly power. [DocSlides listing](https://www.docslides.com/view/what-makes-a-marriage-invalid-very-rev-james-m)

## Fair lines vs. unfair lines

**Fair (true on this archive)**  
- He is a 2011 Memphis priest, a canon lawyer, chancellor since the Holley summer of 2018, judicial vicar, and — since April 24, 2026 — pastor of Holy Rosary while keeping the downtown jobs.  
- Four months later the 23-year principal was gone, and the parish joined the schools office in calling it confidential.  
- He sits ex officio on the board that advises priest moves. That is not the same as the Catholic Schools office.  
- Bishop Talley flew to Ottawa for the dissertation defense. That is a relationship fact, not a plot.

**Unfair (not sourced)**  
- “Fr. Clark fired him.”  
- Any claim of abuse, money, heresy, or a named conspiracy.  
- Using the thesis as if it were about Darren.  
- Treating Holley’s 2018 appointment as proof of later misconduct. Holley named him. Talley kept him. Those are two facts.
