    The public record

    The letters as printed

    19 August. Leadership at Holy Rosary to the parents. Families posted the letter. The Commercial Appeal printed two sentences. No signature appeared in that account.

    Holy Rosary Catholic School · August 19, 2026 · to parents

    Darren Mullis will be departing from Holy Rosary School as our principal, and we wish him the very best moving forward.

    He has an opportunity to continue to serve in the Catholic Schools of Memphis, and we hope that he will pursue that opportunity as his service to our school has been greatly appreciated.

    Signed: not printed. The paper called the sender “leadership at Holy Rosary.”

    Two days later the parish and the schools office spoke as one.

    Joint statement · August 21, 2026 · Holy Rosary Parish and Catholic Schools of Memphis

    We understand that people may have questions, but Darren Mullis’s departure from Holy Rosary is a confidential personnel matter that we are not able to discuss.

    We respect Darren’s privacy, continue to wish him and his family well, and keep them in our prayers. At the same time, we remain focused on supporting the Holy Rosary community and providing stability and continuity for our students and families as we move forward together in faith.

    If you hold the remainder of the 19 August letter — especially a signature — we will publish only what can be shown. We will not invent a name.

    The offices

    The offices that govern this school

    No newsroom has printed that any one person “fired” him. We do not say it. Holy Rosary is a parish school. Particular law of this diocese still names the offices. These are the persons who occupy them. We name the office, not a crime.

    1. Very Rev. James M. Clark

      Pastor of Holy Rosary since 24 April 2026. Still chancellor and judicial vicar.

      Catholic Schools Policy Handbook (May 2018) §207: a parish-school principal “shall be notified in writing, by the pastor, of non-renewal of contract.” The 19 August letter was attributed to “leadership at Holy Rosary.” The 21 August statement was issued in the name of the Parish. The pastor may be addressed with reverence and with the truth.

      Call Email

    2. Dr. Chris Fay

      President / CEO, Catholic Schools of Memphis

      We do not know whether this office had a part in the departure, and we do not assert that it did. No newsroom has assigned the decision to Dr. Fay. He is President of Catholic Schools of Memphis. That office joined the 21 August statement of confidentiality. The diocesan bishop appointed him after a 2024 process on which Darren Mullis served. That is the public record. We stop there.

      His wife, Mrs. Rena Fay, is listed on the Holy Rosary staff directory as Enrollment Coordinator, in Administration, at 4841 Park Avenue. That is a public posting of two offices in one household: one in the parish school, one over Catholic Schools of Memphis. We note the appearance. We do not judge the interior forum. We do not name the children. We do not invite anyone to write or telephone Mrs. Fay.

      The diocese has published that Dr. Fay coached baseball, basketball, and soccer at Holy Rosary, and that their children have attended Catholic schools. This parish received that family. A decision taken against the man who formed those hallways, if it involved this office, would wound communion here. We do not know that it did. We ask him to speak as a Catholic in public office.

      Address Dr. Fay. Do not address his wife. Do not address his children.

    Questions the faithful are asking

    Why is Darren Mullis no longer principal of Holy Rosary?
    The school and Catholic Schools of Memphis have given no public cause. The 19 August letter called it a departure. We do not know the interior of any personnel file, and we do not pretend to. We ask that he be restored as principal. He has held that office since 2003.
    Who is the pastor of Holy Rosary Memphis?
    The Very Reverend James M. Clark, J.C.D., appointed pastor 24 April 2026, remaining chancellor and judicial vicar of the Catholic Diocese of Memphis. We honor the office. We ask him, as pastor, to give a pastoral account.
    Where is Holy Rosary Catholic School?
    4841 Park Avenue, Memphis, TN 38117. East Memphis. Home of the Rams. The parish church is next door at 4851 Park.

    Are you leaving the Church?
    No. We remain in full communion with the Roman Pontiff and with the Bishop of Memphis. We will not omit Sunday Mass. We will not hear it in this parish until the principal is restored.
