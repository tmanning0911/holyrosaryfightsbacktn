Petition to Bishop Talley, Fr. James Clark, and Catholic Schools of Memphis

We ask you to put Darren Mullis back as principal of Holy Rosary Catholic School. He has belonged to this parish since he was two. He has been principal since 2003. A new pastor who does not know this community does not get to erase that. Until you bring him home we will not tithe here and we will not attend here. We will take our families to a parish where the priest knows the people.

Names on the sheet

    What happened

    Aug 19: a letter. Darren is “departing.” No reason. He’s been principal since 2003. Two days later the parish and Catholic Schools of Memphis told the Commercial Appeal it was confidential. Families were already standing at the Catholic Center. The Daily Memphian put the protest on the front of the metro section.

    He grew up here. Ram through eighth. CBHS ’86. Came home, taught, ran youth and religion, then they made him principal. Betty Ann. Their kids. The man in the hallway. Learning Lab, Quest, ANGEL, The Learning Center — those are rooms with children in them.

    April 24: Fr. Clark takes the parish and keeps chancellor and judicial vicar. August 19: Darren is out. That’s the whole story.
