Darren Mullis

Principal · Holy Rosary Catholic School · since 2003

A Ram from the start: Holy Rosary through eighth grade, then Christian Brothers High School, class of 1986. He came home to teach, to lead youth ministry and religious education, and in 2003 the parish named him principal. With his wife, Betty Ann, and their children, this school has been theirs for nearly fifty years.

What is known

19 Aug 2026 — Parents were told Darren Mullis “will be departing” as principal of Holy Rosary Catholic School. No public cause was given. He has held the office since 2003.
